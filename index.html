Privacy Policy for UnderCover Party

Last Updated: November 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

UnderCover Party is designed to protect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users on our servers, except for subscription-related information as described in Section 5 below.

2. Data Collected by Our App

Our app does NOT collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
  • Location data
  • Game data or statistics
  • User-generated content

3. Local Storage

The app stores game preferences and settings locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

4. Subscription Management (RevenueCat)

UnderCover Party offers premium subscriptions managed through RevenueCat. When you subscribe to premium features, the following information is collected and processed:

4.1 Information Collected for Subscriptions

  • Email Address: Used to manage your subscription and send receipts
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status, purchase dates, and transaction details
  • Device Information: Device ID for subscription verification
  • Payment Information: Processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store (we do not store credit card details)

4.2 How Subscription Data is Used

Your subscription data is used to:

  • Process and manage your subscription
  • Verify your premium access across devices
  • Send purchase confirmations and receipts to your email
  • Provide customer support for billing issues

4.3 Email Sharing with Payment Processors

Your email address is shared with payment processors (Apple and Google) to complete transactions and send receipts. This sharing is necessary to process your subscription payment.

4.4 RevenueCat Privacy

RevenueCat acts as a subscription management service. For detailed information about how RevenueCat handles your data, please review:

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Data Processing Agreement
Important: RevenueCat processes subscription data on our behalf. Your email and purchase information are stored securely and used solely for subscription management. You can request deletion of your subscription data by contacting us at arouaystudio@gmail.com.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

UnderCover Party displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob automatically collects certain information to show relevant ads:

5.1 Information Collected by AdMob

  • Advertising ID: A unique identifier for advertising (IDFA on iOS, Google Advertising ID on Android)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version
  • IP Address: Your approximate location
  • Ad Interaction Data: Which ads you view or click

5.2 Purpose of Advertising Data

This data is used by Google to:

  • Display personalized advertisements
  • Measure ad performance
  • Improve ad targeting

5.3 Google's Privacy Policy

AdMob is operated by Google. For detailed information about how Google handles your data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & Privacy
Important: We (UnderCover Party) do NOT have access to the advertising data collected by Google AdMob. Google operates as an independent data controller for advertising data.

5. Your Advertising Choices

You can control personalized advertising at any time:

On Android:

  1. Go to Settings → Google → Ads
  2. Select "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Reset advertising ID"

On iOS:

  1. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  2. Turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  3. Or go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Reset Advertising Identifier

Manage Your Google Ad Data:

Visit My Ad Center to view and manage your advertising preferences with Google.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

For European Users (GDPR):

If you are in the European Union, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request information about your data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Object: Object to data processing
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for personalized ads

For UnderCover Party app data: Simply uninstall the app to remove all local data.

For subscription data: Contact us at arouaystudio@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of your subscription information.

For AdMob advertising data: Visit My Ad Center or contact Google directly.

For California Residents (CCPA):

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt-out of the "sale" of your information

To exercise these rights regarding AdMob data, visit My Ad Center.

7. Age Restrictions

UnderCover Party is intended for users aged 13 years and older.

For users under 16 in the European Union, parental consent is required for personalized advertising. A consent dialog will appear when you first open the app.

8. Data We Share

We share data only with:

  • RevenueCat: For subscription management and payment processing (as described in Section 4)
  • Payment Processors (Apple/Google): Your email address is shared with Apple App Store and Google Play Store to process subscription payments and send receipts
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements (as described in Section 5)

We do NOT sell your personal information to anyone.

9. Data Security

  • All communication with RevenueCat and payment processors is encrypted via HTTPS
  • All communication with AdMob is encrypted via HTTPS
  • Payment card information is never stored in our app - it is handled directly by Apple and Google
  • Your Advertising ID can be reset at any time in your device settings
  • Local app data is stored securely on your device

10. Data Retention

  • Local app data: Stored on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Subscription data: Retained by RevenueCat for the duration of your subscription plus up to 12 months for accounting and legal compliance. You can request deletion by contacting us at arouaystudio@gmail.com
  • AdMob data: Retained by Google according to their policies (typically up to 26 months)

11. Offline Functionality

UnderCover Party works completely offline. However:

  • Advertisements require an internet connection to load
  • Without internet, you may see cached ads or no ads at all
  • All game features work offline

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Significant changes will be communicated within the app.
